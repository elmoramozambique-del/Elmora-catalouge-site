All three are real. Every product, every price and every photograph is the live catalogue, not a mock-up. Search, categories, collections, the product pages and the full-screen photograph all work in each one.
  • They all use only the five brand-book colours: wine, berry, beige, light beige, charcoal.
  • They all use Montserrat. The brand's primary typeface, Volkga, is not in any of them yet — it is not a free font and no font file has reached me.
  • The opening band in Showroom uses a travertine texture made with Higgsfield. It is a material, not a product. No product photograph is ever generated.
